Resident individual: old regime rebate up to Rs 12,500 if total income <= 5,00,000. New regime rebate up to Rs 25,000 if total income <= 7,00,000; above 7,00,000 tax payable is limited to the income exceeding 7,00,000 (marginal relief). No marginal relief in the old regime.
